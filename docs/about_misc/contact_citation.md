## Contact

**LongAIRR** is developed by
[Jonas Schuck](https://github.com/Jonas-Schuck) and
[Katharina Imkeller](https://github.com/imkeller).

Questions can be sent to `Schuck@med.uni-frankfurt.de`. Bugs and reproducible
software problems can be reported through the
[LongAIRR issue tracker](https://github.com/AGImkeller/LongAIRR/issues).
